CastMute

Automatically mute your TV during ads while you cast — hands-free, no remote.

Quiet ad breaks, automatically

Cast a show from your iPhone to your TV, and CastMute silences every ad break the moment it starts — then unmutes the second your show comes back. Set it once and relax.

Automatic muting

Silences ads the instant they begin, so you're never blasted mid-scene.

Hands-free

Auto-connects to whatever TV you're casting to — no setup each time.

Instant unmute

Sound returns the moment your content resumes.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, and it collects no data.

How it works

  1. Cast a show from your phone to your Chromecast or Cast-enabled TV.
  2. Open CastMute — it connects automatically to the TV that's casting.
  3. Ads mute, your show unmutes. Automatically.

Works when you cast from your iPhone to a Chromecast or Cast-enabled TV on the same Wi-Fi. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any streaming service or device maker. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
