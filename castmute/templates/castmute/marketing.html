Rebuilt after looking at apple.com/airtag — the closest analogue there is to this product, a small single-purpose object. Three things came from that and are worth not undoing: 1. LIGHT, not dark. An earlier version of this page was black throughout, on the theory that a dark stage makes a small object look premium. Apple reserves that for Pro hardware; AirTag's hero is #f5f5f7 with black type and the sections below are white. This is a small white-and-orange plastic thing, so it belongs on light. 2. THE HERO DOES ONE JOB: product name, one line of tagline, the object. No description, no price, no button. The explanation, the status and the call to action sit in a separate white section below it. The previous version crammed all of that above the image and the hero had no air in it. 3. FOOTNOTES. Apple superscripts its claims and qualifies them at the bottom. That suits this product unusually well: the copy can say "mutes the break" cleanly while the qualification lives in a footnote, instead of hedging inside every sentence. The honesty is not reduced — the full compatibility section is still here, unsoftened. The concentric rings behind the hero product are the same idea as AirTag's, and are almost free here because the mark already contains arcs.
