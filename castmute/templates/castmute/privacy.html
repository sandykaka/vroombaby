CastMute Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

CastMute does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. CastMute has no user accounts and does not gather, transmit, or store any personal information. No usage data or identifiers are sent to us or to any third party.

Local Network access

CastMute uses your device's Local Network permission solely to discover and control the TV or Cast device you are casting to on your own Wi-Fi network. This communication stays entirely on your local network — it is never sent to us or anyone else.

Third parties

CastMute includes no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking. It does not integrate with any external services.

Children's privacy

CastMute does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
