Frequently Asked Questions

How does CastMute work?

Cast a show from your iPhone to your Chromecast or Cast-enabled TV, then open CastMute. It automatically connects to the TV that's casting and mutes the TV during ad breaks — then unmutes the moment your show resumes.

Why don't I see my TV?

Make sure your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, your TV is on, and CastMute has Local Network access (Settings > CastMute > Local Network). Then cast a show and pull down to refresh the device list.

Which streaming apps work?

CastMute works with many popular ad-supported streaming apps that you cast from your phone. Some services stitch ads directly into the video stream, and those ads can't be detected or muted.

Does it work with my TV's built-in apps?

CastMute only works when you cast from your phone to the TV. Apps opened directly on the TV, or services that use their own casting method instead of Google Cast, won't be detected.

Do I need to keep the app open?

Open CastMute when you start watching. It keeps muting ads even in the background during that viewing session. To save battery, it stops when you leave Wi-Fi or the cast ends — just reopen it next time you watch.

Does CastMute drain my battery?

It only stays active while you're actually watching. When nothing is casting or you leave your Wi-Fi, it goes to sleep so it isn't running in the background all day.

Does CastMute collect my data?

No. CastMute has no account, no sign-up, and collects no personal data. It only talks to your TV on your local network.
